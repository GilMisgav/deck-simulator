Cycle builder

Build the multi-day experience: each day holds its own missions and the decks the player will be served that day. Add days in blocks (3 days now, 4 more later — any mix), compose each day's missions, then Fit decks to pick the decks that maximise the chance of completing that day. Completion odds come from the weighted simulation (Poisson on expected events for counts, exact DP for match-win and streak missions).
